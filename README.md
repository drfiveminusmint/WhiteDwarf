It’s been around a century since the Bureau Wars came to a close; the event most people thought would define their lifetime. After the Bureau of Internal Investigation and their allies were finally put down, the Galaxy could begin to heal.

If only it had been that simple.

The Calamity struck seventy years ago. Its cause was, and continues to be unknown, but its effect was overwhelming and undeniable; the deaths of billions, and the total collapse of galactic civilizations. Systems with a hypergate were particularly badly affected, becoming irradiated and totally uninhabitable for years to come. And yet, somehow, humanity survived.

And so your journey begins. You are one of many pilots wandering the stricken galaxy, trying to find a way for yourself by the light of dying stars. The universe, with all its joys and sorrows, all its horrors and comforts, awaits you, captain. Perhaps, amidst it, you’ll find the answer to the most important question of all.

*What kind of person are you, really?*

# A word of caution

White Dwarf contains many changes from the systems of EV Nova, and breaks many previously established conventions of the EV series. If you’re a veteran of the series, make sure you keep an open mind going in; not everything may be as you expected. Many of these systems changes will be familiar to those of you who played Brave New Void.

For a list of the most notable changes, see here: 
[White Dwarf: Notable Feature Changes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YOvj1UbmXP8iPXC4K85WfIC5Qhqy0e5H6ArgZ1zFtQk/edit#heading=h.qeoumva1mjgm)  

Additionally, White Dwarf’s story explores many heavy and dark themes, some of which may make some players uncomfortable. A full content warning is included in the start of the game at character creation. Do whatever you feel is best for you, and feel free to stop playing if any of it gets to be too much.

# Installation

## Modern
1) Make sure you have [EV Nova Community Edition](https://github.com/andrews05/EV-Nova-CE) installed
2) Double-click the `White Dwarf.nplay` file to launch White Dwarf
## Classic
1) Make a copy of your EV Nova folder (yes, the whole thing)
2) Delete the `Nova Files` folder and replace it with White Dwarf's `Nova Files` folder
3) Launch the copy of ``EV Nova.exe`` in your new folder to play White Dwarf

# Troubleshooting / FAQ

## I can't get the next mission in a string to trigger. 
  
Try leaving the planet and landing again. The Nova engine is kinda finnicky about multiple missions being offered at the same planet and sometimes an invisible mission triggering will stop further missions from being offered. Sometimes, you'll even have to land on another planet and come back.  
  
Alternatively, there could be a delay for the next mission, there could be a combat rating gate, or it could just be a bug (the developer is only human, after all.) If you strongly suspect it to be the latter, feel free to bug the developer (DrFiveMinusMinus#6697) on discord.
## I got a strange message when I captured a ship.

White Dwarf is built around the assumption that you will be flying solo, with your only escorts being fighters or Followers. Capturing escorts will change the balance of combat and will likely make your experience worse. Additionally, large captured escorts will slow down your ship in hyperspace, bypassing the normalized jump times.
## I was told to dominate a planet. What does that mean?

Planetary Domination is a feature in the EV series that is typically used to allow the player to extort planetary governments for money. In White Dwarf, it is used slightly differently; often appearing as part of the story. To dominate a planet, hail it (by pressing ‘Y’ after selecting it on your stellar navigation bar using the number keys) and select the “Demand Tribute” option. The planet should launch its defense fleet, unless it is immune to domination (see below.)
## I can’t dominate this planet. Why?

Not all planets can be dominated in White Dwarf; namely, government capitals and story-relevant planets usually cannot be dominated unless made available by a mission.
## I don’t remember this in the Nova universe!

White Dwarf makes a few lore changes of its own, but most of the new additions to the Nova lore are imported from Brave New Void, my previous work. While you don’t have to have played Brave New Void to understand White Dwarf, those of you who have can assume that everything from BNV is canon in White Dwarf.
